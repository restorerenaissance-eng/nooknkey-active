Bachelorette Weekends · Nashville

The bachelorette house your group chat will never let you forget.

4.98 ★ on Airbnb Airbnb Guest Favorite Superhost Sleeps 12 8 min to Broadway

Crystal chandeliers. Emerald velvet. A fireplace in nearly every room. This is the house that becomes the whole weekend's highlight reel — eight minutes from Broadway, and far enough away that you actually sleep before brunch.

One of our favorite reviews came from a bachelorette party of nine who were left stranded by another host at the last minute. We got them in within hours, and they called it a 10 out of 5. That's how we host every group.

Why groups pick this house

  • Sleeps up to 12 in four bedrooms, plus a blue sleeper sofa in the Parlor
  • Photo-ready rooms: the chandelier foyer, the emerald living room, the pink-and-gold Pink Room
  • Two full baths and plenty of mirrors for getting ready
  • A big, fully fenced backyard for slow mornings and loud mimosas
  • 8 minutes to Broadway, and a 5-minute walk to coffee and brunch on 51st Avenue
  • Self check-in, so you can arrive whenever your flight lands after 4 PM
