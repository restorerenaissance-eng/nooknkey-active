Our Story

An 1890 Victorian in The Nations, brought back to life.

When we found this house, it was the most forgotten Victorian in the neighborhood. A century-old beauty on a corner lot, in the part of Nashville locals call The Nations. Original 1890 mantels, gorgeous crystal chandeliers, and show-stopping twelve-foot pocket doors with brass thumb-pulls.

"We didn't want to flip this house. We wanted to keep its soul."

The decision was easy: not to rebuild the house, but to restore all the charm and beauty of the past. The fireplace tile stayed. The pine floors stayed. We added crystal chandeliers, emerald velvet chesterfields, a brass leopard pedestal because we couldn't help ourselves, and the kind of bedding you sleep eleven hours in.

During the renovation, neighbors stopped by daily to watch the progress. Now they stop to take pictures.

— The Nook & Key

Good Questions

Before you book.

Is it safe to book directly on this website?

Yes. Payments run through Stripe, the same processor used by millions of businesses, and your card details never touch our servers. You're booking with the same host you'd get on Airbnb: Giorgio has been a Superhost for two years. The house holds a valid Metro Nashville short-term rental permit (#2024078967).

What's the difference between booking here and on Airbnb?

Same house, same host, same cleaning team. When you book here there's no platform in the middle, so you deal with us directly, and you get extras like complimentary early check-in when the calendar allows.

How many people can stay?

Up to 12 overnight guests. Groups of 10 or more get a quick approval from us first. Just send your details with the Request Approval button.

Where does everyone sleep?

A king in the Master, a king in the Pink Room, a blue sleeper sofa in the Parlor, and several beds in the upstairs Loft. See the room-by-room layout above.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes. Dogs are welcome with approval, and the backyard is fully fenced. A one-time pet fee is shown at checkout. If you're bringing more than one pet, send us a quick note first.

Is there parking?

Yes, free private parking for multiple cars, right at the house.

Can we host a party or event at the house?

Yes. Weddings, elopements, rehearsal dinners and celebrations are welcome by arrangement. Tell us your date, guest count and plans through the contact form and we will confirm what works. Overnight stays sleep up to 12.

How far is Broadway?

About 8 minutes by car. Coffee, dinner, and ice cream on 51st Avenue are a 5-minute walk.

How do check-in and check-out work?

Self check-in from 4:00 PM with a smart lock, and check-out by 10:00 AM. We send a digital welcome book with Wi-Fi, parking, and our local favorites 24 hours before you arrive.

Can we stay for a few weeks or a month?

Yes, and many guests do: families between homes during an insurance claim or renovation, and people relocating to Nashville. You get a full kitchen, washer and dryer, a workspace, fast Wi-Fi, and a fenced yard. Ask us about monthly rates.

What if something goes wrong during the stay?

Message or call us any time. We answer fast, and we have a local team in Nashville who can be at the house quickly.
